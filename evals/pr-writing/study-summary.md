Sanitized aggregate evidence from a private historical study. No private repository/PR identity, source/diff, raw trace, user path, session identifier, answer key or live label is included. The shipped synthetic fixture cannot reproduce model results. Others can use their own authorized private inputs with an existing supported native harness and retain telemetry locally.

## Larger PR-only validation

- Ten distinct substantive real PRs across five repositories, two paired repeats each, seeded alternating order, sequential writers. Unseen outputs for this method, but repositories already used in development.
- **40 slots, 39 completed, one interrupted/unqualified attempt** retained without retry/replacement. **19 complete pairs, nine fully repeated PRs**; all ten retain at least one matched pair.
- Median reduction in each fully repeated PR's median: **21.7%**, range 5.2–27.5%. Candidate faster **15/19** complete pairs. Four slower candidate results remain, worst 34.5% slower. The incomplete single-pair case is excluded from the primary nine-case estimate.
- Matched 19-vs-19 caller sums: 4306.863→3321.145 s. Input: 31,062,033→17,609,234 tokens (**43.3% lower**); cached: 28,656,000→15,905,408; uncached: 2,406,033→1,703,826; output: 163,537→126,118. Counters accumulate over turns, not unique prompt size; cache is a subset of input. Interrupted usage/time and assessor telemetry unknown, not zero; unmatched candidate cost separately retained.
- Absolute matched-call medians: 221.8→163.2 s; ranges: 152.3–431.4 s and 132.9–309.2 s. This pooled difference is not the same statistic as PR-level 21.7%.
- Candidate collection: 1.35–2.84 s included each run; median tool roundtrips: 27→16.5 over completed calls. Most native time lay outside observed command windows; inference, transport, queue and buffering could not be separated. No mandatory exact-HEAD wait or reviewer-recursion bottleneck demonstrated.

Twenty independent source-only assessors, two per PR, assessed exact neutral outputs after writing. **Each method had three drafts flagged for material omissions** by at least one assessor. Findings included specification/code contradictions and authorization limits; one had fail/uncertain disagreement. All 39 final checker bindings passed, without establishing semantic correctness.

Usefulness votes: candidate 21, direct 13, tie 4 across 38 comparable judgments. However, both assessors saw the same orientation: 33 preferred B, one A, four ties. Candidate occupied B in 12/19 comparable pairs. Strong side skew limits inference; no reversed-orientation calibration or reliable preference advantage established. Source-cited material findings remain separate, uncertainties intact.

A later deliberately selected five-pair human subset across different repositories included ordinary and omission/disagreement cases: **source-first 3, direct 1, tie 1**; outputs described as close. Summed time: 1010.128→801.569 s (**20.65% lower**), candidate faster 4/5. One chosen repeat per PR, **not random holdout**, not pooled with prior human labels or decisive superiority. Preferences do not erase factual omissions.

## Rejected directions and qualifications

A separate acquisition-model/fact-sheet compiler took about 310 s before a setup failure; including recovered writing its execution was about 531 s against 273 s direct. Not adopted. Earlier batched auditing established no quality-preserving win. A frequently cited 424.648 s observation was **Audit, not Draft**. Checker microbenchmarks are not end-to-end drafting gains.

An earlier small source-first stage showed modest PR gains but mixed transfer: documentation faster yet direct preferred; research slower and direct preferred. Those profiles were not expanded here. PR gains do not establish speed/quality benefits for other writing profiles.

These were fresh historical replays over frozen local inputs, not genuine warm implementation continuations, first-install baselines or cache resets. Shared acquisition/setup/review/grading were separate overhead, partly uninstrumented. Heavy VM work waited until writers finished; other contention remained possible, rolling host load high and not CPU/queue telemetry. No busy/slower sample excluded or corrected. Repeats/repos correlated, samples small, interruption cause and terminal cost unknown. Evidence supports a promising PR-specific process change, **not universal no-quality-loss or latency guarantees**.
