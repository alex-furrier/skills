# Research handoff before planning

Use this pattern when authorized work already has research and planning owners.
It is optional; a settled fix or a task one owner can handle directly need not
add helpers or a checkpoint.

Ask the research owner for its consequential findings, context-specific signs of
a good result, and a compact provisional synthesis the user can correct. Existing
`goalify` can help; a separate synthesis role is optional. The handoff ends at
research and the synthesis, leaving expansion to the planning owner.

Publish that synthesis in the working conversation before starting the planning
owner. Give the planner the complete research, source limits and established
constraints as well as the published brief. Keep supporting detail available
through links so the short brief preserves the depth behind it.

Continue independent authorized work. Carry corrections and unresolved material
assumptions forward; pause only work that depends on an unanswered question.
Reuse existing feedback, without adding a confirmation ritual or a required
report format.
