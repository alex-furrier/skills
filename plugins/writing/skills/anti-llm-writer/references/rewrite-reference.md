# Rewrite reference

Use this reference to identify writing that is inflated, vague, or mechanically repetitive. These patterns are prompts for careful revision, not evidence of authorship or quality.

- Replace unsupported importance claims with the concrete effect the source establishes.
- Remove filler transitions and conclusions that add no decision, fact, or next action.
- Prefer concrete actors, actions, constraints, and examples over abstract uplift.
- Keep useful headings, comparisons, technical terms, quotations, commands, warnings, and qualifications.
- Make the lightest change that improves clarity without inventing facts or simulating an individual voice.

The result should remain recognizably the author’s work and preserve all authority and safety boundaries.
