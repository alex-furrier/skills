# Contextual drafting examples

Use these examples to diagnose meaning, not to enforce replacement words.

## Inflated claim

Before: `This revolutionary workflow provides robust capabilities.`

After: `The workflow retries failed imports and records the rejected rows.`

The revision names behavior. Keep a broad term when it is the exact product,
protocol, or domain wording.

## Forced structure

Before: three equal sections with three bullets each, created only for symmetry.

After: one paragraph for the main behavior and a list only where readers need
separate steps.

## Protected meaning

Keep bytes exact only where a machine consumer or explicit user/owner contract
requires them, such as command syntax, identifiers, link destinations, or a
marked exact passage. Do not change the meaning, qualifications, billing or
authentication facts, safety constraints, or authority boundaries merely to
lower a readability score or remove a deterministic finding. Route any factual
or constraint-changing judgment to the destination owner.
