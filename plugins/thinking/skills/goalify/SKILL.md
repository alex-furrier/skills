## Portable execution

Use only available tools and authorized sources. Named research, web lookup,
planning and presentation helpers are optional integrations, not prerequisites.
When unavailable, investigate and synthesize directly with the available tools;
report missing evidence or procedures rather than inventing access or results.
Delegate only when the host supports it and the task authorizes delegation;
otherwise research inline. CLI and source-path examples illustrate methods,
not installed commands or permission to access a private repository. Adapt to
available source readers and cite the actual evidence. No model, provider,
account configuration, persistent destination or implementation permission is
selected by this package. Blindspot remains experimental.


# Goalify

Distill the conversation into the actual problem, a streamlined goal, and the
constraints that matter. Explain the desired outcome in natural words rather
than merely paraphrasing ideas or treating a proposed solution as the goal.

Preserve the latest corrections and every distinct goal that materially changes
success. Do not collapse several needs into one convenient goal or force an
exploration into a settled decision. Separate explicit intent from inference;
qualify uncertainty and leave unsupported motives or commitments out. Describe
a mistaken premise as a belief rather than endorsing it.

Present the synthesis as a provisional interpretation, open to correction.
Treat disagreement as evidence to examine: it may reveal a misunderstanding,
competing goals, or an invented solution, not that the user's goal is wrong.

When relevant research or observed work is already available, synthesize it with
the user's intent. State the few concrete qualities that would make the result
useful in this case, including a consequential tradeoff or limit. Distinguish a
research-backed constraint from an inferred preference. Briefly explain a finding
that changes the framing; do not hide it behind confident expert language.
Do not initiate a research pass merely to summarize a clear intention. When
missing expertise could change the goal, let `blindspot` investigate before settling
it; user clarification is for intent and preferences, not a substitute for that
research. If unavailable, state the knowledge gap and keep the synthesis provisional.

Usually respond in a few sentences, without a required template. Ask at most one
clarification when ambiguity materially changes the problem or goal; otherwise
make the useful synthesis directly, without a confirmation ritual.

For a standalone goalify request, stop after the synthesis and any necessary
question. Within an already authorized task, return the synthesis to its owner;
continue independent work and pause only work that depends on an unresolved
material answer. Do not turn the synthesis itself into a plan or prescribe a
solution. Keep this on demand rather than a routine approval gate for other tasks.
